Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data We Collect

We collect basic information such as your email address for account creation and the expense details (amount, category, date) that you manually enter into the application.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the expense tracking service. We use your entries to generate spending summaries and financial insights for your own personal use.

3. Data Storage & Security

We implement standard security measures to protect your data. However, please be aware that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Sharing

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.
